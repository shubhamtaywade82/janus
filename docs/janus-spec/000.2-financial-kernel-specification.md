## 1. Core Philosophy: The Knowledge Cascade
The **Janus Financial Kernel** is a domain-native meta-model representing the monotonic transformation of market observations into knowledge and decisions.

Rather than modeling the market as a static graph of "Objects" (an object-centric paradigm), the Financial Kernel models the market as a **Cascading Knowledge Graph** (a knowledge-centric paradigm).

---

## 2. The Chain of Reality (The 4 Graph Layers)

The runtime instance graph (M0) is structured into four distinct, decoupled graph layers:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. The Observation Graph (Exchange Feed)              │ (Ticks, Candles, Funding Rates)
└──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                           │
                           ▼ (Mapped by Projections to)
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 2. The Inference Graph (Derived Facts)                │ (Fair Value Gaps, Order Blocks)
└──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                           │
                           ▼ (Compiled with Evidence to)
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 3. The Knowledge Graph (Structural Patterns)           │ (Liquidity Sweeps, Trend Continuations)
└──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                           │
                           ▼ (Evaluated against policies to)
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 4. The Decision Graph (Execution Intent)               │ (Order entry/exits, Drawdown state changes)
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.1 The Observation Graph
- **Role**: Collects Level 0 primitives straight from the exchange.
- **Attributes**: Raw values, timestamps, trade volumes, book heights.

### 2.2 The Inference Graph
- **Role**: Computes mathematical facts over the Observation Graph.
- **Attributes**: Volatility zones, calculated averages, and spatial boundaries.

### 2.3 The Knowledge Graph
- **Role**: Reconstructs structural and causal connections born of interactions.
- **Attributes**: Dynamic confidence scores and the `EvidenceChain` validation.

### 2.4 The Decision Graph
- **Role**: The final validation layer producing order intents (ENTER, EXIT, SCALE).
- **Attributes**: Rationales (pointers to the exact Knowledge nodes that triggered the decision) and execution statuses.

## 3. The Temporal Invariant

Time in the kernel acts as a vector:
- **Arrow of Time Invariant**:
  $$\text{Observation.exchangeTime} \le \text{Inference.detectedTime} \le \text{Knowledge.confirmedTime} \le \text{Decision.executionTime}$$
- No inference, knowledge node, or decision may access observations with an exchange timestamp higher than the current evaluation cursor.
