## 1. Core Philosophy: The Lifecycle of Connections
Just as market objects have lifecycles, **relationships (edges) have lifecycles**. A relationship between two objects is not static; it changes in state, strength, and confidence over time. 

By modeling this lifecycle explicitly, the Replay Engine can deterministically reconstruct the graph at any historical microsecond, and the Strategy Engine can query only connections that are active and confirmed.

---

## 2. The Relationship Lifecycle State Machine

Every relationship in the Janus Knowledge Graph progresses through these states:

```
    NON_EXISTENT
         │
         ▼ (Detect Interaction)
      FORMING
         │
         ▼ (Confirm Physics)
      ACTIVE
         │
         ▼ (Trigger Events)
     CONFIRMED
         │
         ▼ (Time Decay / Opposite Price Action)
     WEAKENING
         │
         ▼ (Fails Threshold / Outlived Session)
     RESOLVED  ───> [Outcome Attached]
         │
         ▼ (Archive)
     ARCHIVED
```

### State Definitions
- **`FORMING`**: An interaction is registered (e.g. price touches FVG), but the relationship is not yet confirmed.
- **`ACTIVE`**: The relationship is registered in the Hot Graph (Graphology), has a baseline weight, and is queryable by strategies.
- **`CONFIRMED`**: Additional evidence confirms the link (e.g., price rejects from the FVG with high volume, confirming mitigation). Weight/confidence are updated to peaks.
- **`WEAKENING`**: No new interactions occur, or price action drifts away. Time decay reduces the relationship's strength.
- **`RESOLVED`**: The relationship has reached its end (Outcome attached: e.g., `SWEPT`, `MITIGATED`, `EXPIRED`).
- **`ARCHIVED`**: Unloaded from the Hot Graph and stored in PostgreSQL for AI narrative profiling and historical audit.

---

## 3. Transition Rules, Triggers, and Guards

Lifecycle transitions are driven by **Relationship Systems** in response to market events.

```yaml
# FVG Mitigation Confirmation
transition:
  from: ACTIVE
  to: CONFIRMED
  trigger: PriceRejectsFromZone
  guard:
    policy: VolumeRejectionConfirmationPolicy
    minRvol: 1.5
  action:
    command: UpdateRelationshipStrength
    newStrength: 0.95
    newConfidence: 0.90
```

---

## 4. Relationship Outcomes

When a relationship reaches the `RESOLVED` state, one of these outcomes must be attached to it:
- **`COMPLETED`**: The interaction successfully concluded (e.g. FVG fully filled).
- **`INVALIDATED`**: Causal thesis disproven (e.g. price sweeps an OB that was supposed to hold).
- **`EXPIRED`**: The link decayed due to time/distance (e.g. correlation between assets normalized).
- **`CANCELLED`**: The parent order/workflow was aborted.
